# XC for Silo 0.8.0

- Calibrate a channel’s statistics delay with the period and clock visible on its broadcast. Recent feed observations provide conservative timing; ambiguous or older clocks explain how to try again.
- Opt into Game moments for scoring, injury timeouts and finals. Alerts use mature delayed data, deduplicate plays, and suppress hidden, paused or rewound playback. Other followed games share the existing cached statistics endpoint with at most three additional games polled every 30 seconds.
- Follow players from their box scores using provider athlete IDs. Follows, delays and saved splits belong to the active Silo profile; game suggestions use a team observed within the last day.
- Game switching weighs personal follows and existing game rankings, gives five seconds’ notice in automatic mode, and retains Cancel, Stay here, manual-choice cooldowns and delayed-ending safeguards. Off remains the default.
- Open a suggested Sports split, select two or four channels and save up to eight named presets. Layout changes keep playing videos attached. Browser connection limits apply, with configured provider-account capacities also honored in XC.

Includes regression checks for profile isolation, concurrent preference saves, delayed finals and late multiview session responses, plus desktop, narrow-phone and enlarged-text browser fixtures. No new third-party dependency or SDK protocol change.
